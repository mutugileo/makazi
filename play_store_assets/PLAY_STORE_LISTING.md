# Makazi — Google Play Store Listing & Asset Specifications

## 1. Store Listing Copy

### App Name (Max 30 characters)
`Makazi: Tenant Rent & Bills` *(27 characters)*

### Short Description (Max 80 characters)
`View itemized rent & water bills, confirm M-Pesa payments & get PDF receipts.` *(78 characters)*

### Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)
Makazi is the modern tenant companion for residential properties in Kenya. Stay on top of your monthly rent, water meter readings, payment confirmations, official PDF receipts, and maintenance requests—all in one secure app.

**CLEAR MONTHLY BILLING**
• View itemized monthly bills covering rent, water meter units (previous reading, current reading, and rate per unit), garbage fees, and any balance brought forward.
• Track your billing cycle (issued on the 4th of every month) and grace period dates with zero guesswork.

**SIMPLE M-PESA & BANK PAYMENT CONFIRMATION**
• Copy your property's M-Pesa Paybill, account number, or bank details in a single tap.
• Paste your M-Pesa or bank confirmation message directly in the app for fast verification by your property manager.
• Edit or remove unconfirmed payment messages if you ever paste the wrong SMS.

**INSTANT PDF RECEIPTS & FULL STATEMENTS**
• Download and share official PDF receipts as soon as your payment is confirmed.
• Access your complete running tenancy statement and security deposit record anytime.

**PHOTO-BACKED REPAIRS & MAINTENANCE**
• Report plumbing, electrical, or general unit issues in seconds and attach photos.
• Track repair status in real time—see the assigned technician (fundi) name, phone number, and scheduled visit time.

**DIRECT PROPERTY MANAGER MESSAGING**
• Chat directly with your landlord, caretaker, or property management office.
• Receive real-time notifications for payment confirmations, rent reminders, and maintenance updates.

**BUILT FOR PRIVACY & SECURITY**
• Protected by a 4-digit PIN and optional biometric unlock (Fingerprint / Face Unlock).
• Submit digital move-out (vacation) notices and track your tenancy records effortlessly.

*Note: Makazi works with properties managed on the Makazi Property Management platform. Your landlord or property manager provides your sign-in credentials when you move in.*

## 4. Data Safety Questionnaire Summary

- **Data Encryption:** Yes, all data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS.
- **DataCollected:**
  - **Personal Info (Name, Phone number, Email address):** Collected for account authentication and tenancy identification (App functionality, Account management).
  - **Financial Info (Payment confirmation SMS / transaction reference):** Collected when tenant submits payment confirmation (App functionality).
  - **Photos and Videos:** Collected optionally when tenant attaches a photo to a repair request (App functionality).
  - **Messages (In-app messages):** Messages between tenant and property manager (App functionality).
- **Data Sharing:** No data is sold or shared with third-party advertisers.
